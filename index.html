    Lock automatically

    iPhone apps aren't allowed to lock the screen, but an iPhone Shortcut can. Make it once:

    1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and name it Shelf Play Locked.
    2. Add three actions: Open URLs (tap its URL and pick Shortcut Input), Wait 3 seconds, Lock Screen.
    3. Come back here and turn it on.

    If Shortcuts says it can't find the shortcut, check the name is exactly Shelf Play Locked, or turn auto-lock off here.

    Gemini key

    Free. It lets Gemini watch videos for summaries, notes and Anki cards. Make one at aistudio.google.com/apikey (Create API key, then Copy) and paste it here.

    The key stays on this phone. Shelf only sends Google the video's link, its own instructions and your short mark note. Google may use free-tier requests to improve its models.
